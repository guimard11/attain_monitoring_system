# Automating MSME Performance Reporting with Microsoft Fabric and Power BI

## Overview:
The Success Project supports more than 25,000 micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs) by improving their access to financial services, strengthening business capacity, and enhancing market competitiveness through partnerships with local firms and financial institutions.

To address reporting inefficiencies and improve decision-making, a modern data solution was developed using Microsoft Fabric and Power BI. The project automates data collection and reporting workflows through a scalable data pipeline and interactive dashboard, enabling leadership teams to monitor project performance, track KPIs against targets, and generate timely reports for sponsors and government stakeholders.

The solution also provides actionable insights into grant performance, including MSME onboarding, business support activities, job creation, and enterprise profit growth.

## Business Understanding
After the first year of implementation, the Success Project leadership team faced significant challenges in efficiently monitoring project performance and measuring impact across supported enterprises. Project data was fragmented across multiple departments, limiting visibility, reporting accuracy, and timely decision-making.

While implementing partners reported achieving 100% of their MSME support targets, operational reports revealed a 15% gap between targeted and registered businesses, despite full grant allocation reported by the finance team. These inconsistencies highlighted the need for a centralized and reliable reporting system.

This project addresses these challenges by developing an integrated reporting solution that centralizes project data into a single platform, enabling stakeholders to monitor activities, track KPIs, improve reporting accuracy, and support data-driven project management.

## Analysis Framework & Visualization
A business intelligence and reporting framework was designed to improve project monitoring, reporting accuracy, and performance analysis across all stakeholders.
The solution included:
1.	Reviewing the project logic model, results measurement framework, and KPI structure to align reporting with strategic objectives. 
2.	Designing a centralized data collection system aligned with operational workflows and reporting requirements. 
3.	Deploying six digital survey tools to capture data on MSME registration, business support activities, financial assistance, job creation, and revenue growth. 
4.	Building an automated data pipeline using the Medallion Architecture (Bronze, Silver, Gold layers) to streamline data ingestion, transformation, and integration processes. 
5.	Developing an interactive Power BI dashboard to monitor more than eight KPIs and deliver actionable insights for data-driven project management and decision-making.

## Conclusion & Business Impact
The automated reporting solution — combining a scalable data pipeline and an interactive Power BI dashboard — was successfully validated and adopted by the project leadership team to support operational and strategic reporting needs.
The solution is now used to:
1.	Monitor project activities and implementing partner performance on a weekly basis. 
2.	Generate quarterly donor reports with improved accuracy and efficiency. 
3.	Support semi-annual reporting to organizational headquarters and key stakeholders.

Within six months of implementation, reporting gaps were reduced by 90%, significantly improving data quality, reporting consistency, and on-time submission rates. The platform also enabled continuous monitoring of MSME performance, revealing a 10% profit increase among 50% of supported enterprises.
This project demonstrates how modern business intelligence solutions can strengthen project governance, improve data-driven decision-making, and enhance impact measurement at scale.
